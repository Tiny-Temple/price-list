      How to read these prices

      • Prices are per linear metre of board, ex VAT, the same for everyone whether you order one board or a pallet.
      • The price per square metre uses the coverage, the width each board covers once fitted, so it already allows for gaps, overlaps and tongues. For PAR, Fine Bandsawn, board on board and Rainscreen you can type your own gap or overlap and the price per square metre follows it.
      • Boards are supplied in random lengths from stock unless you tell us the length you would ideally like.
      • Setup fees apply under 20 m²: £60 for machining each product, and £150 for each coating colour. Every product in the same coating counts together towards its 20 m², so one colour across several profiles pays the coating fee once at most.
      • Sizes split from a larger piece are sold in multiples of the pieces it splits into. In twos: 42 x 42 boards, 42 x 20 Thermal Radiata, Thermal Pine and Western Red Cedar, 66 x 20 Frake and Thermal Radiata, and 92 x 20, 100 x 23, 142 x 20 and 150 x 23 Scottish larch. In fours: 42 x 20, 47 x 23, 66 x 20 and 73 x 23 Scottish larch.
      • Battens, screws and ventilation strip are priced with your quote, and so is delivery: the delivery figure on this page is an estimate. Collection from the mill at Pomathorn Farm, Penicuik EH26 8PJ is free, any time 9am to 5pm Monday to Friday.
      • Standard lead time is two weeks from payment.

      To get a quote, send us the species, profile, size, finish and the area or the elevation drawings, with the delivery address if you would like it delivered.
